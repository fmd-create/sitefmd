01 · FMD CONNECT

Great service is only the beginning. Conversion matters.

FMD CONNECT structures customer journeys and supports ongoing conversations with business context, operating rules and clear paths to conversion.

  • Hospitality: guest inquiries, policies, rooms, guest relationships and booking opportunities.
  • Services: customer service and scheduling for clinics, dental practices, beauty businesses and professional services.
  • Capabilities subject to scope and integration include alerts, CRM, multiple channels and conversion tracking.

Features and integrations are defined according to each implementation scope.

02 · FMD NYRA

Fewer lists. Better commercial context.

FMD NYRA is a B2B commercial intelligence platform designed to discover companies, enrich business information, identify decision-makers and prioritize opportunities that match an ideal customer profile.

  • Company research and qualification.
  • Business data, digital signals and fit analysis.
  • Human-approved, supervised outreach.

Specialized applications: FMD NYRA intelligence can be adapted to new markets, with scope and data sources assessed for each project.

03 · FMD WEB STUDIO

Your digital presence, intelligently crafted.

FMD WEB STUDIO is our intelligent production system for websites, landing pages, social posts and carousels. Every project starts with understanding the business, its audience, visual identity and brand personality. Technology streamlines production; strategy and creative direction make every experience distinctive.

  • Responsive corporate websites and landing pages focused on user experience and conversion.
  • Social media posts and carousels with a consistent visual identity.
  • Conversion-focused copy, creative direction and SEO foundations for web projects.

A studio built for distinctive digital experiences — not a factory of identical templates.

04 · FMD MOTION

Content brought to life. From idea to production.

FMD MOTION brings together custom audiovisual production and Content Factory, our AI-powered solution for structured, recurring content creation.

  • Custom production: commercial videos, Reels, product showcases and campaigns.
  • Subscription-based Content Factory: access to a recurring production workflow, depending on the features included in each plan.
  • Digital avatars and custom formats, subject to technical testing and project scope.

Deliverables, available features, duration, usage rights and subscription terms are defined in commercial proposals.
